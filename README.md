## 🚀 Curious About What I'm Currently Building?

**[Deskling](https://github.com/Pritika04/deskling)** 🪴

A little personal digital pet that lives as a widget on your desktop! I'm building Deskling as a companion for college life, with gentle reminders to eat, drink water, take breaks, and check in with yourself.

It's evolving from a simple habit tracker into something more like a journaling and chat companion — a tiny friend to make overwhelming days feel a little less overwhelming.

More soon! :)
